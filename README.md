# healthcare_vs_tech-wages
What is the average weekly wage for the Healthcare industry versus the Information (Tech) industry in California?

**Rationale for the Project**
A startup in San Francisco has just secured hundreds of millions of dollars in funding. To secure the very best talents, the board is looking to pay 15% more than the market currently pays; A hospital has been receiving pay increment requests from some of their best staff across multiple departments and doesn't want to lose them - so the admin staff want to know if the market is paying more than they're currently offering;
A college student with a CS major considering whether to pursue opportunities in the healthcare sector or tech.
**Utility: Useful for career selection, competitive salary benchmarking and talent acquisition strategy.

**Code summary**
The code contained accurately determines what the average worker earns in the healthcare industry versus the tech industry.

**Dataset(s) Used**
The dataset used is the official QCEW (Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages) data for California from 2004-2007, gotten from data.gov website.
